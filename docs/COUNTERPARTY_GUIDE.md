# Counterparty Guide (Who Signs What)

Effective date: 2026-05-18
Owner: Eric

This is practical guidance, not legal advice.

## What is a counterparty?
A counterparty is the other legal person or company signing an agreement with you.

## For the docs created today

### NOTICE.md
- Typical type: unilateral policy notice.
- Counterparty needed: no.
- Signature needed: optional owner acknowledgment only.

### COPYRIGHT.md
- Typical type: ownership statement.
- Counterparty needed: no.
- Signature needed: owner signature.

### THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md
- Typical type: compliance inventory.
- Counterparty needed: no.
- Signature needed: owner sign-off optional, useful for audit trail.

### CLAIMS_MATRIX.md
- Typical type: internal publication policy.
- Counterparty needed: no.
- Signature needed: owner sign-off recommended.

## When you do need a counterparty
You need one when creating a bilateral agreement, such as:
- enterprise customer agreement
- NDA
- contractor agreement
- contributor agreement
- licensing deal with another company

## If you are solo right now
Given your current statement that you are the only contributor:
- most core policy docs are unilateral and owner-signed only.
- add counterparties later when you onboard customers, vendors, or collaborators.

## Signature workflow suggestion
1. Finalize text.
2. Sign owner sections yourself.
3. Convert to PDF.
4. Store signed copies in secure records folder.
5. Use e-sign platform only for bilateral agreements.
