**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement")** is entered into as of __________, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between **Eric Breon / Molly-Core** ("Disclosing Party") and **__________________________** ("Recipient").

## 1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship involving the Titan Echo B2B compression technology (the "Purpose"). In connection with this Purpose, Disclosing Party may share proprietary algorithms, compression manifests, and technical documentation, and Recipient may share sample datasets ("Confidential Information").

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:
*   **From Disclosing Party:** Titan Echo source code, Component S0/T1-T4 methodologies, trade secret threshold constants, and the "Cradle" orchestration logic.
*   **From Recipient:** Sample AI interaction logs, JSON/Proto datasets, and internal system architecture details.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS
The Recipient shall:
*   Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose of evaluating a "Prove It" Pilot.
*   Protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care as its own confidential information.
*   Not reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to derive the source code of the Titan Echo engines.
*   Restrict access to employees or contractors with a "need to know" who are bound by similar confidentiality obligations.

## 4. THE "PROVE IT" PILOT PROVISO
Disclosing Party agrees that any sample data provided by Recipient for the Purpose of a compression audit will be processed locally. No data will be uploaded to external cloud servers, and all raw samples will be deleted by the Disclosing Party upon delivery of the final Integrity Report, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement remains in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.
