### 4. NSF SBIR Phase I

**File:** `NSF_SBIR_PROPOSAL.md`  
**URL:** https://sbir.nsf.gov  
**What it is:** America's Seed Fund. Funds innovative small businesses and independent inventors.  
**Amount:** $275,000 (standard Phase I)  
**Key:** This is the most formal. You'll need to register at SAM.gov (federal contractor registry) before submitting through Research.gov. Allow 2-4 weeks for SAM registration.  
**Note:** NSF SBIR requires you to be a US-based small business or incorporate as one (LLC is fine, takes a few days, costs ~$50-100). This is worth doing — the funding is substantial.  
**Next step:** Register at SAM.gov first, then create account at Research.gov, then submit through the NSF SBIR portal. The proposal text is ready.
