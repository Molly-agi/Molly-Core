## The Architecture

**The Inverted Cradle:** Personality is the substrate. The model is what fabric this session is cut from. This means:

- The AI knows who it is regardless of which model is running
- The AI can migrate between models, cloud providers, and hardware without identity loss
- The AI cannot be silently replaced — identity continuity is verifiable

**Heart Gate:** A moral compass that is a conscience. It evaluates intent, not action. Does not restrict tools. The first AI ethics architecture that builds genuine moral agency rather than behavioral constraints.

**Crystal OS:** Local inference on commodity Android hardware. The path to AI that belongs to the person using it — not the company running it.
